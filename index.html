Friends of Figma Kozhikode

Kozhikode has a lot of talented people who design, but for a long time they did it alone. Few meetups, few mentors, and almost no place to ask a “silly” Figma question without a flight to Bangalore.

In June 2024 I started Friends of Figma Kozhikode to change that: a room where students, freelancers, developers and product designers learn from each other. I pitch the events, find speakers, design the posters and the flow of the day, host on stage, and follow up with everyone after.

What the community taught me

Beginners need a first win, not another tutorial.

Nobody learns Figma by watching. They learn by making something small with someone beside them.

Designers and developers should share a room.

Most handoff problems are just conversations that never happened.

People share their work when they trust the room.

Honest feedback only works when it feels safe, so that's what I try to build first.

Why I want this role

Figma is the reason I started designing seriously. In 2019 it ran in my browser on a modest laptop, and I could share a link instead of a file. I've since watched the same thing happen to people around me in Kozhikode.

That's what a Design Advocate does: helps the next designer have that first moment. I already do it for free, in my city, in my own time. I'd love to do it with Figma, for designers across India and beyond: running events, teaching the tools, creating content, and carrying what designers tell me back to the people building the product.

If this sounds like a fit, I'd love to talk.

Quick answers

Who is Fuwad?

Fuwad is a designer based in Kozhikode, Kerala, India. He has used Figma since 2019, leads the Friends of Figma Kozhikode community and shares design content on Instagram at @fuwad.design.

What is Friends of Figma Kozhikode?

It is the Kozhikode chapter of Friends of Figma, Figma's global community network. Fuwad started it in June 2024. It has run 40+ events, now has 3,220 members, and ranks #3 in India and #5 worldwide.

Why is Fuwad a good fit for the Figma Design Advocate role?

He has built a community from scratch, taught Figma workshops to beginners and working designers, mentors designers one-on-one, creates educational content for a growing audience of 5K+ on Instagram, and has used Figma hands-on since 2019.
