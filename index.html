Hi, I'm Fuwad. I design products, bring designers together, and make learning design a little more accessible.

I opened Figma for the first time in 2019 and haven't really closed it since. Today I lead Friends of Figma Kozhikode, mentor aspiring designers one-on-one, and share what I learn with 5K+ people on Instagram at @fuwad.design.

I'm applying for the Design Advocate role at Figma. This page is my case for why: not a pile of screens, but evidence of the community I've built, the sessions I've taught, and the designers I've helped.

02 · Building community

Friends of Figma Kozhikode

Kozhikode has a lot of talented people who design, but for a long time they did it alone. Few meetups, few mentors, and almost no place to ask a “silly” Figma question without a flight to Bangalore.

In June 2024 I started Friends of Figma Kozhikode to change that: a room where students, freelancers, developers and product designers learn from each other. I pitch the events, find speakers, design the posters and the flow of the day, host on stage, and follow up with everyone after.

What the community taught me

Beginners don't need more tutorials, they need a first win.

So sessions now end with everyone shipping something small, together.

Designers and developers learn better in the same room.

Joint sessions on handoff cut the “it didn't look like this in Figma” conversations.

The best feedback comes from honest peers.

Portfolio reviews and show-and-tell are now a fixed part of our meetups.

06 · Why Figma

Why I want this role

Figma was the reason I started designing seriously in 2019. It was free to try, it ran in the browser on my modest laptop, and I could send a link instead of a file. I've since watched the same thing happen to people around the globe.

That's what a Design Advocate does: help the next designer have that first moment. I already do it for free, in my city, on my own time. I'd love to do it with Figma, for the wider community across India and beyond: running events, teaching the tools, creating content, and carrying what designers tell me back to the people building the product.

If this resonates, I'd love to talk.

I'd love to be Figma's
voice in the community.
